Privacy

Last updated November 3, 2026.

Summary

Azentix MacTune collects no data. It has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no account and no server. Nothing you do in the app leaves your Mac.

What stays on your Mac

MacTune keeps a few small files in ~/Library/Application Support/AzentixMacTune:

  • per-app traffic totals for the last 14 days, and your limits and internet switches;
  • Hotspot Guard and Limited Launcher settings;
  • the last cache scan, so it doesn't rescan for an hour.

Delete that folder to remove them; it contains nothing else.

The network filter

The optional filter decides for each connection whether the app that opened it may use the internet. It sees the app's identity and the connection, never stores them, and doesn't inspect, record or forward what is sent. It runs only on your Mac.

Permissions

The only link that leaves the app is the azentix.net link in the sidebar, which opens in your browser.
