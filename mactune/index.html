Network control

Your data plan, your rules

MacTune ships a small Network Extension filter. Blocking an app doesn't quit it — its traffic simply stops, and comes back the moment you allow it.

Per app

Internet switch and daily limits

Click any app's Limit & access cell. Turn its internet off, or give it a daily budget.

  • Notification at 80% and when the limit is reached
  • Need a bit more? +10 MB, +100 MB or +1 GB for today only
  • Start counting from now — no surprise "limit reached"
  • Local traffic (127.0.0.1, emulators) never counts
How limits work →

On a hotspot

Hotspot Guard

Tethered to your iPhone? Turn on Hotspot Guard and only the apps you allow can use the internet. Everything else keeps running, quietly offline.

  • One list with a switch per app
  • Turns on by itself on Personal Hotspot and Low Data Mode networks
  • Apps launched by an allowed app inherit its access
