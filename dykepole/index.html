The Wild Fields are calling

Old legends, honest duels

Every round you choose where to strike and what to guard. Your opponent does the same at the same moment, so the one who reads the enemy better wins.

Five heroes

Kozak, kharakternyk, molfar, mavka and opryshok, each with their own abilities and style of fighting.

Eight lands

From the outskirts of the Sich through the Carpathians and the Polissia marshes to the Chornobyl Zone, Zmiinyi Island and Nava.

Creatures of folklore

Upyrs, vovkulaks, the chuhaister, witches and Zmiy Horynych himself, each with its own temper and loot.

Gear and the forge

Sabres, bartkas, chainmail and charmed vyshyvankas drop in battle, are sold at the fair and are tempered at the forge.

Your own zymivnyk

Build a farmstead in the steppe, sow flax and wheat, grow char-zillia and brew mana elixir while you are away.

Online duels

Challenge real players and climb the rating. With no opponent around a bot steps in, and offline the whole game still works.
