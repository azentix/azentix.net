Support

Help with Dyke Pole: Legends.

Українською: Підтримка

Contact us

Write to support@azentix.net. We read every message and usually answer within two working days. It helps a lot if you include your iPhone model, iOS version, the game version and what you did just before the problem.

Progress and devices

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, with a backup copy in your anonymous player record on the server. The game has no login, so for now progress cannot be moved to another device, and after deleting the game it may not be possible to restore it.

Do I need the internet?

No. Adventures, the homestead and fights against bots work offline. Online duels, the rating, friend codes and purchases need a connection.

Purchases

I paid but the fern flower did not arrive

Each purchase is confirmed by our server before it is paid out. If the connection dropped, the purchase stays pending and is completed the next time you open the game online, so nothing is lost. If it still has not arrived after a restart, write to us with the order number from Apple's receipt email.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: open reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

Can I win by paying?

Fern flower speeds up progress, but duels are decided by the choices you make each round.

Online duels

Nobody is found for a duel

If no player is waiting, a bot takes the challenge so you never wait long. Rating changes are calculated by the server.

My opponent left

A player who stops making moves forfeits the duel after a short wait.

Friend codes

Open Sich › Friends to see your code and share it. A newcomer who enters it gets a gift, and you get a reward for each friend. A code can be entered once per player, and you cannot enter your own.

Language

The game is available in Ukrainian, English, Polish, German and Spanish. Change it with the globe button on the Sich screen.

Your data

See the privacy policy for what the game stores and how to have it deleted.
