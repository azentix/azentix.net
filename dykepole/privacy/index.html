Summary

Dyke Pole: Legends does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. There is no registration, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. To make online duels, the rating and purchases work, the game keeps an anonymous player record on our server. This policy explains what is in it. The game is published by Azentix.

What the game stores

When the game first connects to the internet it creates an anonymous account: a random identifier that is not tied to your Apple ID or to who you are. Under that identifier we keep:

  • Your hero: the hero name you typed, class, level, equipment, homestead and other game progress, so the server can run duels fairly.
  • Duels: who fought whom, the moves each side made and the result. Your hero name, level and rating are shown to your opponent.
  • Rating: your duel rating, calculated by the server.
  • Friend code: your invite code and which codes were redeemed, so rewards are given once.
  • Purchase records: for each purchase, Apple's transaction identifier, the product and the time, so a purchase is paid out exactly once.

Our hosting provider, Google Firebase, also processes technical data that any internet service sees, such as the IP address and device type, to sign the app in and protect the service from abuse. We do not use it to identify you.

The hero name is free text. Please do not put personal information in it.

What it never collects

The game does not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, microphone or camera input, advertising identifier, or browsing history. It contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Where it is kept

Your progress is saved on your device. The anonymous player record described above is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions) in a data centre in the European Union. Google acts as our processor; see Firebase's privacy information. We do not sell data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

We keep the record while you play. Records of accounts that have not been used for a long time may be removed.

You can play against bots without a connection. In that case nothing is sent to the server.

Purchases

Packs of fern flower are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. The game sends Apple's signed receipt to our server only to confirm that the purchase is genuine. Apple's privacy policy covers the transaction itself.

Friend codes

If you share your friend code, the game opens the standard iOS share sheet. The game does not read your contacts and does not know whom you sent the code to. When someone enters your code, the server links the two anonymous accounts to give both rewards.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the progress saved on your device. To erase the anonymous record on the server, write to support@azentix.net and include your friend code (Sich › Friends in the game) so we can find the record. We delete it within 30 days. You can also ask us for a copy of the record or to correct it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
