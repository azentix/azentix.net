Frequently asked questions

Short answers about Pulse.

General

What does Pulse do?

It records which app is in front on your Mac and for how long, and shows it as day, week and month charts, a table of every app, and today's time in the menu bar.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

Does it need any permissions?

No. Pulse doesn't ask for Accessibility, Screen Recording or Full Disk Access.

Can it show which tab, document or Figma file I worked on?

No, by design. Pulse only knows which app was in front, which keeps it simple and private.

Does it slow my Mac down?

No. It checks once every few seconds and does almost nothing in between.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. It follows your Mac's language, or you can pick one in Settings › Language.

How time is counted

Does time count when I'm away?

No. After a few minutes without keyboard or mouse input (you choose how many in Settings › General), Pulse stops counting, and it doesn't count the minutes since your last input either. Locking the screen and sleep stop it straight away.

I watched a video and the time is missing

Without input, Pulse can't tell watching from being away. Raise the idle time in Settings › General if you often watch long videos.

Does Pulse need to stay open?

It counts while it runs, in the menu bar or with its window open. Turn on Open Pulse at login so it's always counting. Closing the window keeps it running; Quit stops it.

Can I leave some apps out?

Yes: Settings › Privacy › Don't track these apps. You can also pause tracking from the menu bar panel or the sidebar.

How are categories chosen?

From the category each app declares, plus a built-in list for popular apps. Apps without one are shown as Other.

Trial and purchase

How much does it cost?

Every feature is free for 7 days. After that, Pulse Forever unlocks it for good with a one-time purchase (US$4.99, or the local equivalent). There's no subscription.

Why is the trial an in-app purchase?

The 7-Day Free Trial is a free item in the App Store. That's how Apple asks apps with a one-time purchase to offer a trial, and it remembers when your trial began across reinstalls.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pulse keeps recording in the background, but the dashboard and menu bar panel stay locked until you buy Pulse Forever. Nothing is lost: your history is all there once you unlock.

Can my family use it?

Yes. Pulse Forever supports Family Sharing.

I bought it on another Mac

Click Restore Purchases on the welcome screen or in Settings › Purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.
