How to play

Last raccoon standing wins

The deck holds one stick of dynamite fewer than there are players. Draw it without Cutters and you are out. A game takes a few minutes.

Play what you like

On your turn play as many cards as you want: look at the top of the deck, shuffle it, skip your turn or make the next player take two.

Draw to end the turn

Every turn ends by drawing a card, unless a card let you off. That draw is where the dynamite hides.

Defuse or go boom

Cutters defuse the dynamite, and you slide it back into the deck wherever you choose. No Cutters: you blow up and watch the rest.

The deck

Eleven cards, no rulebook needed

Every card says what it does. Raccoons do nothing alone, but a matching pair steals a random card from a player you pick.

Ways to play

Alone on the train or loud with friends

Versus bots, offline

One to four bots, no connection needed. They take a moment to think, so you can follow every move.

Ranked quick match

Play real opponents and climb the leaderboard. The server runs the game, so nobody can peek at the deck.

Private rooms

Create a room, share the six-character code and play with friends, up to five players. Empty seats can be filled with bots.

Emotes

Laugh when somebody blows up. Reactions pop up for the whole table, and the bots answer back.

Make it yours

Card backs, avatars, table themes and emote packs are bought with coins you earn by playing.

Fair and quiet

No ads, no subscriptions, no account to create. Coin packs are optional one-time purchases.

Questions?
