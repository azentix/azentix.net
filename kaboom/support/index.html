Support

Help with Kaboom: Raccoons & Dynamite.

Українською: Підтримка

Contact us

Write to support@azentix.net. We read every message and usually answer within two working days. It helps a lot if you include your iPhone model, iOS version, the game version and what you did just before the problem.

Rules in short

The deck holds one stick of dynamite fewer than there are players. On your turn play any cards you like, then draw one. If you draw Dynamite and have Cutters, you defuse it and put it back anywhere in the deck. Without Cutters you are out. The last player left wins. The full list of cards is behind the question mark on the home screen.

What do raccoons do?

One raccoon does nothing. Two matching raccoons played together steal a random card from a player you choose.

What happens when I am out?

You can keep watching. In games against bots and in private rooms you also see the hands of the players still in. In ranked games the hands stay hidden.

Online play

How long is a turn?

45 seconds. When the time runs out, the other players can skip your turn: the game then makes a move for you so the table is not stuck.

How do I play with friends?

Open Online, create a room and share its six-character code. Friends enter the code under Join by code. A room holds two to five players, and the host can fill empty seats with bots.

How does the rating work?

Quick match games between real players change your rating, and the leaderboard shows the top 50. Private rooms and games with a single human player do not change the rating.

What if I leave a game?

The game asks before you leave. Leaving a game in progress counts as a loss, and the others play on.

Coins and the shop

How do I get coins?

You start with 100 coins and earn more for every finished game, more for a win. Games against bots are rewarded for the first ten games each day. Coin packs can also be bought, but nothing in the game requires it.

What can I buy with coins?

Card backs, avatars, table themes and emote packs. They change how the game looks and how you react, not your chances of winning.

Is there a subscription?

No. The game has no subscriptions and no ads.

Purchases

I paid but the coins did not arrive

Each purchase is confirmed by our server before the coins are added. If the connection dropped, the purchase stays pending and is completed the next time you open the game online, so nothing is lost. If the coins still have not arrived after a restart, write to us with the order number from Apple's receipt email.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: open reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.

Progress and devices

The game has no registration. Your coins, items, level and rating belong to an anonymous player record that is tied to this installation. Deleting the game may make that record impossible to restore, and progress cannot be moved to another device yet.

Do I need the internet?

No. Games against bots work offline. Online games, the shop, the leaderboard and purchases need a connection.

Language

The game is available in Ukrainian, English, Polish, German and Spanish. Change it with the globe button on the home screen.

Deleting your account

Open Profile and choose Delete account. This removes your profile, rating, coins and items from the server right away and cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for details.
