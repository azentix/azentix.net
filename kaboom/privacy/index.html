Summary

Kaboom does not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location. There is no registration, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. To make online games, the rating, the shop and purchases work, the game keeps an anonymous player record on our server. This policy explains what is in it. The game is published by Azentix.

What the game stores

When the game first connects to the internet it creates an anonymous account: a random identifier that is not tied to your Apple ID or to who you are. Under that identifier we keep:

  • Your profile: the player name you typed, the avatar you chose, level, rating, coins, the items you own and the ones you use.
  • Games: the rooms you played in, the moves made and the result. Your player name, avatar and emotes are shown to the other players at the table, and your name, avatar and rating appear on the leaderboard.
  • Purchase records: for each purchase, Apple's transaction identifier, the product and the time, so a purchase is paid out exactly once.

Our hosting provider, Google Firebase, also processes technical data that any internet service sees, such as the IP address and device type, to sign the app in and protect the service from abuse. We do not use it to identify you.

The player name is free text. Please do not put personal information in it.

What it never collects

The game does not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise or approximate location, photos, microphone or camera input, advertising identifier, or browsing history. It contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Where it is kept

Your language, player name and chosen look are saved on your device. The anonymous player record described above is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions) in a data centre in the European Union. Google acts as our processor; see Firebase's privacy information. We do not sell data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

We keep the record while the account exists. You can play against bots without a connection. In that case nothing is sent to the server.

Purchases

Coin packs are sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. The game sends Apple's signed receipt to our server only to confirm that the purchase is genuine. Apple's privacy policy covers the transaction itself.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself: open Profile in the game and choose Delete account. Your profile, rating, coins and items are removed from the server right away. If the game is no longer installed, write to support@azentix.net and we will help. You can also ask us for a copy of the record or to correct it.

Purchase records (Apple's transaction identifier, without your profile) are kept after deletion so the same receipt cannot be used twice. Rooms you played in keep the player name and avatar you had at the time.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
